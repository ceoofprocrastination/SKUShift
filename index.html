The simplest way to move inventory between marketplaces

Selling on more than one channel is one of the fastest ways to grow an online store, but every marketplace wants your product data in a different shape. Amazon expects flat files with its own column names, eBay uses File Exchange templates, Etsy has a Shop Manager import, and Shopify relies on a product CSV with handles and variant rows. An inventory CSV converter removes the manual copy and paste that usually stands between you and a new sales channel.

What an inventory CSV converter does

A converter reads the columns in your source file, such as SKU, title, description, price, quantity, brand and image URLs, and maps each one to the field the destination marketplace requires. Instead of retyping hundreds of listings, you upload one file and receive another that is ready to import. Along the way, the tool can calculate the total number of SKUs, the combined value of your stock and your average price, which gives you a quick sanity check before you publish anything.

How to convert your product CSV

  1. Export your catalog from the current marketplace as a CSV file.
  2. Drag the file into the upload area, or click to browse your computer.
  3. Review the SKU count, inventory value and the three-row preview.
  4. Select the marketplace you want to sell on next.
  5. Download the converted file and import it in the destination dashboard.

Why validate before you import

Most import failures come from small data problems: duplicate SKUs, blank prices, quantities written as text, or stray commas inside titles. Checking your totals first helps catch these issues early. If the SKU count is lower than you expect, rows may have been merged or skipped. If the average price looks unusually high or low, a currency symbol or decimal separator may be the cause. Spending two minutes on review can save hours of cleanup after a failed bulk upload.

Marketplace-specific tips

Amazon: every listing needs a unique SKU, a product type and, in most categories, a UPC, EAN or brand registry exemption. Keep titles under the category limit and avoid promotional phrases.

eBay: File Exchange requires an action column and a category ID. Fixed price listings need a format, duration and condition, so check those fields after conversion.

Etsy: listings are limited to thirteen tags and require a who-made, what-is-it and when-made declaration. Handmade, vintage and supply rules apply, so confirm that your products qualify.

Shopify: products are grouped by handle, and variants sit on additional rows. Make sure options such as size and color are consistent from row to row.

Walmart: approval is required before selling, and item feeds must follow the current Walmart spec for your category, including shipping and fulfillment details.

Keep your data private and your stock accurate

Your inventory is a business asset, so it should not be sent to an unknown server. This converter is designed to process files locally in your browser, which means your costs, prices and supplier details stay on your device. Remember that a converted file is a snapshot. Once you list on several channels, quantities can drift quickly, so consider a multichannel sync tool or a regular reconciliation routine to prevent overselling and cancelled orders.

Best practices for multichannel growth

Start with your best sellers rather than your whole catalog. Test one marketplace, confirm that fees and shipping rules make sense, and then expand. Keep a master spreadsheet with a stable SKU for every product, because consistent SKUs make conversions, reporting and returns far easier. Finally, tailor titles and descriptions for each channel after the conversion, since buyers on different platforms search in different ways.

Frequently asked questions

Is the inventory CSV converter free to use?

Yes. The tool is free and does not require an account. Advertising helps cover the cost of keeping it online.

Is my product data uploaded to a server?

No. Files are read and converted inside your browser, so your catalog stays on your device.

Which file formats can I upload?

Standard comma-separated .csv files. If your data is in Excel, use Save As and choose CSV UTF-8 first.

How is total inventory value calculated?

Each row's price is multiplied by its quantity, and the results are added together. The average price is the mean unit price across all SKUs.

Will the converted file import without errors?

Core fields are mapped for you, but each marketplace has category-specific rules. Review required fields such as category, condition and identifiers before importing.
